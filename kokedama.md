## The Essence
**Eyebrow:** THE ESSENCE

# What is Kokedama?

Kokedama is a Japanese technique in which a plant is grown in a prepared ball of growing medium wrapped in natural moss. The moss ball holds the roots and moisture, and forms the visible surface of the piece.

A Kokedama has no glass enclosure, so its care differs from that of a terrarium. The moss ball needs regular checking, and watering depends on how it feels rather than on a fixed routine.

---

## Care Principles
**Eyebrow:** CARE PRINCIPLES

# Five essential care principles.

Light, water, humidity, placement and maintenance keep a Kokedama healthy. Each is straightforward, and together they cover everyday care.

### Light
Keep your Kokedama in bright, indirect natural light. Avoid prolonged direct afternoon sunlight, which can scorch the moss and stress the plant.

### Water
Water according to the condition of the moss ball, not a fixed schedule. When it feels noticeably lighter or begins to dry, soak it in clean water for 10–15 minutes. Let the excess water drain completely before returning it to its place.

### Humidity
Kokedama generally prefer moderate to high humidity. In dry indoor conditions, light misting around the moss can slow drying. Misting does not replace soaking when the moss ball needs water.

### Placement
Display your Kokedama on a desk, shelf or table, or hang it if it has been prepared for hanging. Choose a position with bright, indirect light and gentle airflow, away from heaters and air-conditioner vents.

### Maintenance
Remove damaged or dead leaves as they appear. Rotate the Kokedama occasionally so the plant receives light evenly. Check the moss ball regularly to make sure it does not dry out completely.

## Precautions
**Eyebrow:** PRECAUTIONS

# Things to Avoid.

A few simple precautions will help keep your Kokedama healthy over time.

### Overwatering
Wait until the moss ball feels slightly lighter before soaking again. Excess moisture can damage the roots and the moss.

### Standing in water
After soaking, let the Kokedama drain thoroughly. Do not leave the moss ball sitting in a pool of water.

### Direct afternoon sunlight
Strong direct sunlight can scorch the moss and stress the plant. Bright, filtered or indirect light is better.

### Heaters and air conditioners
Dry airflow and sudden temperature changes disturb the moisture balance of the moss ball. Keep the Kokedama away from direct heating and cooling.

### Letting the moss dry out completely
Check the moss ball regularly. Water it when it feels noticeably lighter or the moss begins to look dry.

## Common Questions
**Eyebrow:** COMMON QUESTIONS

# Frequently Asked Questions.

Answers to common questions about caring for your Sylva Auris Kokedama.

### How often should I water my Kokedama?
There is no fixed schedule. Soak the moss ball when it feels noticeably lighter or begins to dry. Depending on indoor conditions, this may be roughly every 1–2 weeks, but always check the moss ball rather than relying on the calendar.

### Can Kokedama live indoors?
Yes. A Kokedama can be displayed indoors when the plant receives bright, indirect light and the moss ball is kept suitably moist. Choose a position away from harsh direct sunlight, heaters and air-conditioner vents.

### Does the moss need special care?
The moss should not be allowed to dry out completely. Check it regularly, and soak the Kokedama when the ball feels noticeably lighter or the moss begins to dry. Light misting can help in dry indoor conditions.

### Can I hang my Kokedama?
Yes, if it has been prepared with a secure hanging method. If yours is designed for a shelf or tabletop, keep it on a stable surface rather than adding an improvised support.

### How long will a Kokedama last?
A Kokedama is a living plant arrangement, so how long it lasts depends on the plant, the growing conditions and the care it receives. Suitable light, attentive watering and occasional maintenance help it stay healthy over time.
